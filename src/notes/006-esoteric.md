_Esoteric_ describes something meant for, or understood by, only a small group
of people with specialized knowledge. It often implies ideas that are obscure,
technical, or intentionally kept private.

The word comes from the Greek _esōterikos_, meaning "inner" or "belonging to an
inner circle", originally used for teachings reserved for initiated students of philosophers[^1].

## Why I looked it up

I came across a GitHub thread[^2] that described a potential bug as not being an
“esoteric concern”. That phrasing made me pause, since I only had a rough
sense of what the word implied, so I looked it up.
