To _rinse and repeat_ (or more commonly _wash, rinse, repeat_) is an idiom about
doing something again in the same way[^1]. Also used as a sarcastic metaphor for
following instructions slavishly without thought or that a task should be
repeated _ad infinitum_.

It's origin is (most likely) as instructions on shampoo bottles, but I was
unable to find any real sources on this topic. The story goes that this
instruction was added with the intended idea of increasing shampoo usage and
sales. Over time, the phrase migrated into everyday language as a metaphor
for repetition in any context.

## Why I looked it up

I was making a joke about instructions on how to breathe and became curious
about where the saying originally came from.
