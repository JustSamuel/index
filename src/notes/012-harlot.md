A _harlot_ is a prostitute or a promiscuous woman. However, the word originally had a very different meaning and was applied to men.

The term entered English around the 13th century from Old French _herlot_ or _arlot_, where it meant a beggar or rogue. For centuries, it was used to describe male low-status individuals such as jesters.

According to Etymonline, the shift in meaning to refer to a promiscuous woman began in the early 15th century and became common by the 16th century, solidifying its place in the language when it was used in early English Bible translations as a less vulgar term for "whore"[^1].

## Why I looked it up

In the movie "Wake Up Dead Man" a character is referred to as the _Harlot Whore_. After looking up the definition of harlot it seems to be a tautology.
