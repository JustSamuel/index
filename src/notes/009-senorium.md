A _sensorium_ is the full collection of an organism’s sensory experience:
everything a being takes in and interprets through its senses, along with the
mental processes that make sense of those inputs. The word entered English in
the mid-1600s from Late Latin, built on the stem _sens-_ meaning "sense".[^1]
Earlier uses sometimes referred to the brain as the "seat of sensation", but
today the term is used more broadly to describe how different organisms, people,
or cultures rely on different mixes or "ratios" of senses in how they
perceive the world.[^2]
